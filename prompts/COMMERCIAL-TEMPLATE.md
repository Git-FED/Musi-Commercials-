# MUSEAI COMMERCIAL — GENERIC TEMPLATE (scene-only)

## Part 1 — The brief (human only; never goes into the video prompt)

Product:        [PRODUCT NAME]
Concept:        [ONE SENTENCE — what the product is / does]
Final sign:     [PRODUCT NAME]
                [CONCEPT LINE — short, plain]
                [URL — spelled exactly]
Look:           [LOOK — e.g. dark, calm, animated]
Duration:       [SECONDS — e.g. 10.0]
Format:         [e.g. 1280x720 landscape]
People:         [none / only in the last 25% / midpoint — pick one]

## Part 2 — The generation prompt (this is what gets sent)

Generate a [DURATION]-second [FORMAT] commercial for [PRODUCT NAME].

Scene-only: physical scenes the camera can film. No concept words, no explanation, and no on-screen text until the ending.

Opening ([0s–25%]):
[Describe a physical place and physical things in it. No people, no screens.]

Middle ([25%–75%]):
[Describe one physical action with physical things. People only if the brief allows them, and only where the brief puts them. Still no screens anywhere.]

Ending ([last 25%]):
The camera settles on the big framed sign and holds still:
"[PRODUCT NAME]"
"[CONCEPT LINE]"
"[URL]"

The URL is the largest line, alone on its line, spelled exactly right. The URL appears nowhere before the ending.

Look: [LOOK]. Dark, calm, animated. Zero screens anywhere in the video — no phones, monitors, laptops, TVs, or tablets. No logos anywhere in the video — no brand logos, app icons, or watermark logos. The final sign's text is not a logo.

## Part 3 — Hard rules

1. Zero screens.
2. Exact URL: largest line on the final sign, alone on its line, and absent before the ending.
3. Physical scenes only.
4. The concept sentence never goes into the generation prompt.
5. All text is saved for the final sign.
6. No logos. Any logo visible in any frame is defective.
