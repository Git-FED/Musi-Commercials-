# Prompt Rules

1. **Scene-only.** Describe places, objects, and physical actions. No concept words, claims, or explanations.
2. **Concept kept out.** The one-sentence concept lives in Part 1 only and is never sent to the generator.
3. **Text saved for the final sign.** Product name, concept line, and URL are the only text, and only at the ending.
4. **Zero screens.** No phones, monitors, laptops, TVs, tablets, or glowing displays in any frame.
5. **Exact URL.** It must be spelled exactly, be the largest line, and sit alone on its line.
6. **People placement.** People appear only where the brief allows them. Report earlier appearances as deviations.
7. **Looping.** If looping is requested, the ending must return to the opening shot. A held sign does not loop.
8. **No logos.** No brand logos, app icons, or watermark logos in any frame. The final sign text is text, not a logo.
