# Scene-Only vs Concept

Putting a concept into a generation prompt makes the generator invent what the concept looks like. That often produces screens, garbled text, wrong branding, or irrelevant imagery.

A scene-only prompt gives the camera physical things: a counter, paper forms, envelopes, a brass tray, and glowing dots. Meaning stays in the brief and returns only as exact text on the final sign.

The result is calmer, more controllable, and easier to verify.
